## Elevator (about 40 words)

Charmdesk is an all-ages Roblox clicker where the fantasy is collecting, not paying for power. Glint buys upgrades. Robux buys trails, operator skins, and rooms. Prestige resets the grind and leaves the desk intact. Launching with 48 shop cosmetics and a weekly limited.

## Portfolio case (about 160 words)

Charmdesk started as a systems problem: how do you ship a Roblox clicker that can fund live ops without loot boxes or pay-to-win?

The loop is simple. Clicks and Operators earn Glint. Prestige 1 lands around 90 minutes. Cosmetics never expire; only shop listings do. Monetization is eight Robux price points (49–14,999), a 50-tier Battle Pass after week 3, and one limited drop every Wednesday. Receipts are server-authoritative: the client prepares a purchase, the server stores intent, `ProcessReceipt` grants or issues a voucher. No gacha. No prestige speed for sale.

The look is Charmdesk: 3-head vinyl mascots, enamel-pin outlines, sticker FX, ink / cream / gold. Art is studio-only and rated All Ages.

I designed the economy, catalog, DataStore schema, and receipt path. Implementation is in progress. Weekly cosmetics are the real product after launch.
